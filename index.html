3. Classify Proposals into Five Portfolio Types

After the initiative inventory is complete, classify each proposal once according to its primary purpose. This prevents the same initiative from appearing in several categories.

A

ENTERPRISE STRATEGIC PROGRAMMES

Enterprise-wide programmes with significant cross-divisional impact and shared outcomes (e.g. Financial Sustainability, Customer Transformation, Digital Foundation, Workforce Transformation, Governance Stewardship, Data & Analytics and TAHARA).

B

MANDATORY GOVERNANCE / COMPLIANCE INITIATIVES

Initiatives required by law, regulation, policy, audit, risk or institutional obligation (e.g. DPIA, cybersecurity, contract governance, ESG reporting and compliance management).

C

ENABLING PROGRAMMES

Shared capabilities that enable several programmes (e.g. Enterprise Architecture, cloud foundation, data governance, change management, workforce capability and the HOME31 Platform).

D

DEPARTMENTAL IMPROVEMENTS

Department-focused or BAU improvements that strengthen operations without becoming separate enterprise programmes (e.g. SOPs, reporting, training and workflow improvements).

E

PROPOSALS REQUIRING FURTHER MANAGEMENT DECISION

Proposals that require further analysis or Management direction before they can be classified and sequenced (e.g. government support assumptions, TAHARA options, organisation structure, cloud model and major CX investment).
