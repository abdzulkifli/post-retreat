3. Sort Proposals into Five Decision Categories

A

ENTERPRISE STRATEGIC PROGRAMMES

Enterprise-wide programmes with major cross-divisional impact (e.g. Financial Sustainability, CX Transformation Stack, Cloud & Digital, Workforce Transformation, Governance Stewardship, Data & Analytics, TAHARA).

B

MANDATORY GOVERNANCE / COMPLIANCE INITIATIVES

Required by law, policy or regulation (e.g. DPIA, Cybersecurity, Contract Governance, ESG reporting, and compliance management).

C

ENABLING PROGRAMMES

Enable multiple programmes (e.g. Enterprise Architecture, Cloud Foundation, Data Governance, Change Management, Workforce Capability and the HOME31 Platform).

D

DEPARTMENTAL IMPROVEMENTS

Primarily departmental or BAU improvements (e.g. training, SOPs, dashboards, reporting and workflow improvements).

E

PROPOSALS REQUIRING FURTHER MANAGEMENT DECISION

Require a strategic decision, trade-off or funding choice (e.g. government support assumptions, TAHARA options, new roles or structures, cloud model, CX investment).
